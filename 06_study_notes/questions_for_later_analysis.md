# Questions For Later Analysis

## Data Questions

- Which countries drive the high-inflation association?
- Are missing controls concentrated in specific regions or income groups?
- Would excluding crisis or hyperinflation episodes change the main coefficient?

## Model Questions

- Does the inflation result survive country-specific trends?
- Does the result differ between advanced, emerging, and low-income economies?
- Are there nonlinearities when money growth is very high?

## Identification Questions

- Can we find a stronger external instrument?
- Is the current instrument plausibly exogenous after country and year fixed effects?
- Do placebo failures point to anticipation, common shocks, or bad timing?

## Writing Questions

- Should the final report emphasize Lucas-style long-run facts or the panel audit?
- Which figure best communicates the failed gate without burying the main association?
- What is the cleanest one-paragraph conclusion for an interview or portfolio reader?
