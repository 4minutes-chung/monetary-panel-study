# What We Learned

## Core Result

Money growth and inflation move together strongly across countries in this panel.

## GDP Result

GDP growth does not have a robust relationship with money growth in the main FE/IV specifications.

## Identification Result

The current IV setup is not strong enough for confident causal claims. The conservative relevance gate narrowly fails, the strong-IV threshold fails, and one placebo diagnostic is significant.

## Best Interpretation

This is a strong associational macro panel project with useful IV diagnostics, not a finished causal identification paper.

## Next Analysis Questions

- Can a better external instrument improve first-stage strength?
- Are results driven by high-inflation countries or specific regions?
- Would monetary aggregates behave differently by income group or exchange-rate regime?
- Are short-run LP-IV inflation paths stable under alternative controls and sample windows?
