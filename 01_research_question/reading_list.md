# Reading List

Read for framing, not for adding every branch into the final project.

## Core

1. **Lucas, R.E. (1996)** - Nobel lecture. Main motivation: long-run monetary neutrality / money-inflation fact.
2. **McCandless, G.T. Jr. & Weber, W.E. (1995)** - Cross-country long-run money and inflation facts. Closest empirical benchmark for Notebook 01.
3. **De Grauwe, P. & Polan, M. (2005)** - Money-inflation relationship is strongest in high-inflation countries and weaker in low-inflation regimes.
4. **Sargent, T.J. & Surico, P. (2011)** - Money-inflation link weakens when inflation is low and expectations are anchored.

## Short-Run And Regime Context

5. **Stock, J.H. & Watson, M.W. (2007)** - Inflation forecasting instability and changing inflation dynamics.
6. **Nakamura, E. & Steinsson, J. (2018)** - Identification in macroeconomics; useful guardrail against causal overclaiming.
7. **Jorda, O. (2005)** - Local projections; relevant only for the LP-IV appendix and possible distributed-lag extensions.

## Inflation Targeting Appendix

8. **Bernanke, B.S. & Mishkin, F.S. (1997)** - Inflation targeting framework.
9. **Roger, S. (2010)** - Inflation-targeting adoption narrative and dates.
10. **Mishkin, F.S. & Schmidt-Hebbel, K. (2007)** - Inflation targeting evidence.
11. **Ball, L. & Sheridan, N. (2005)** - Skeptical view on inflation targeting.
12. **Lin, H-F. & Ye, X. (2009)** - Matching/selection evidence on inflation targeting.
13. **Callaway, B. & Sant'Anna, P.H.C. (2021)** - Staggered DiD warnings.
14. **Sun, L. & Abraham, S. (2021)** - Dynamic treatment effects and event-study heterogeneity.

## Appendix And Guardrails

15. **Stock, J.H. & Yogo, M. (2005)** - Weak-instrument language.
16. **Lucas, R.E. (1980)** - U.S. low-frequency appendix only, not the main project frame.
17. **Atkeson, A. & Ohanian, L.E. (2001)** - Inflation forecasting background; no longer part of the main notebook spine.
18. **Gali, J. & Gertler, M. (1999)** - Phillips-curve background; archived from the current main analysis.
